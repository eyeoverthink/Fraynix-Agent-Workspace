Use the experience browser

  1. Open History in the toolbar. Search by request, title, outcome or ID.
  2. Filter Chats, Workflows or Steps; use Needs attention for interrupted/cancelled work.
  3. Select an original record. Its readable outcome and full original record stay distinct from a fresh recheck.
  4. Follow Connected work, recheck the evidence, or open the checked saved result.
  5. Expand the archive controls only when you want to free eligible completed slots. Each limit stays unchanged.
