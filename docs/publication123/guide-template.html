What counts as evidence

Every approach repeats three times in a fresh project. A pickup is counted only from the actual game outcome. Failed completion or damage prevents an economic recommendation.

The threshold uses the largest observed extra time divided by the smallest extra pickup gain. Three runs do not establish a confidence bound, and another computer or course may differ.

72 repeated workflows: 36 development, 24 new-scene, 12 software-rendering repeats. The pilot is excluded. Default: full. No automatic strategy promotion.
