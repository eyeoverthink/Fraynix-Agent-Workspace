## Archive contract

The authored-level v2 export retains the exact original destination source text. The reviewed digest must match those bytes. Java removes the single approved appended object and its new visual asset where applicable, then compares the remaining scene with the destination. The final geometry and authored properties must also match the supplied GLB. The reviewed prop, pose, collider, selected floor, and route are checked before local replay.

The viewer continues to read v1 authored-level evidence by checking the supplied final scene, GLB and aisle. The older format lacks the original destination source text. The dedicated contact-deck exchange retains its previous validation and native replay protocol.

## Independent check

The recipient runs both contact trials and the player clearance envelope in isolated local physics, and then repeats the checks. The original hit and miss tolerances remain in force. An authored replay also requires a clear aisle with the archived length. Recipient receipts stay separate from the imported bytes, and their stored comparisons are recomputed when the viewer reopens.

The replay verifies enacted behavior of the supplied scene. Original browser observations remain qualified author evidence. The exchange creates no editable scene or authorization.
