A useful negative result

The proposed pooled policy did not pass its declared usefulness gate. It avoided all misses but refused 48 of 60 tasks. The fixed margin completed one more task. No controller or model weights were promoted.

Utility per task: success +1; miss −2; refusal −0.15; each executed action −0.02. These are declared study costs, not measured real-world economics. A goal is displacement at 0.75 seconds, not a resting position.
