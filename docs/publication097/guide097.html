What remains enforced

Every native source, plan, forecast, actual trace, contact and outcome check is retained. The independent inspector checks original compressed bytes, exact committed prefixes, recovery forecasts, one world commit per completed goal, saved procedure defaults and persistence after reopening. An external clock measures complete recovered requests; Java's cross-boot timing remains unavailable and ineligible for calibration.

Bank size, world history, direction and warmup change together. Both arms receive the same initial copied state and absolute requests; later states are measured rather than assumed equal. Extra full audits may prime caches and are charged. There is no new physical observation, LLM inference, trained cost selector or weight training in this edition.
