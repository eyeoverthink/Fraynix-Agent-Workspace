Why the approach changed

The old controller jumped over a falling mushroom, then reached a gap without enough momentum and lodged at the far corner. Read-only contacts and before/after positions expose the failure. The new controller projects a short item intercept and defers its optional jump while the mushroom falls, preserving existing edge and landing checks.

This prevents the measured failed approach. It does not establish escape from every already-wedged state. Reported requested velocity is distinct from measured displacement.

Six transformed reference scenes per renderer now require simple and revised control to finish. Four unsupported images per renderer still refuse. Known original sprites and bounded two-gap layouts remain the scope. Zero LLM calls in these Java-routed requests; no new model training claim.
