# Edition 086 — decide before fitting

## Question

Can a cheap, conservative admission check avoid an unprofitable calibration fit while retaining every native, report, commit and saved-skill check?

The gate concerns **new fitting**. Existing comparison tasks are sunk work, reported separately. A caller can also budget additional comparison runs before collecting them; this edition does not automatically launch collection. Checking an existing fitted model at task execution remains unchanged.

## Implementation boundary

- New Java service outside the existing procedure/controller implementation identity. Prove compiled equality before reusing edition085 measurements. If any procedure identity changes, stop and collect fresh compatible evidence.
- Read only bounded owned cost summaries for preflight. They are proposals, not verified motion evidence. Mixed implementations, runtimes, procedures, saving workloads, duplicate goals, unsupported counts or invalid budgets fail closed.
- Require two distinct tasks, three independent pairs per task. For each group, mean simple minus mean memory; lower empirical saving is the minimum group mean minus the largest sum of simple/memory ranges, with a 500 ms floor. This is an admission heuristic, not a confidence interval or future guarantee.
- Explicit horizon 1–256 comparable future tasks; finite positive fitting and per-task-check budgets; 0–16 additional comparison runs. Forecast future investment as fit budget plus additional runs times the largest observed complete-task cost. Admit only when conservative net future saving exceeds 125% of that investment. Report historical comparison cost separately.
- Default: six tasks, 120 seconds fit budget, three seconds checking per future task, no new comparison runs. These are declared planning allowances, not measured deadlines or automatic cancellation.
- Negative or unsupported admission never fits, mutates a world or replaces a saved calibration. Default UI recommends simple control and invalidates any prior target confirmation. Positive admission calls the original full verifier/fitter; no skipped evidence checks. Keep an explicit manual research route only in the isolated test runtime for the unconditional baseline.
- Persist bounded decision receipts, including inputs, reason, measured preflight/fit durations and errors. Ordinary review and previews do not load native traces or an existing model. No promotion from header inspection.
