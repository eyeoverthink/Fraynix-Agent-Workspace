# Prop pose from two unmarked views

[[README-249|Edition 249]] · [[docs/publication249/plan|Reproduction]]

Overhead matching recovered two off-grid Blender poses, with 5 mm floor position error and 1° heading error each. The second angle independently agreed with the fitted headings. Rotation-only, translation-only and symmetric-heading controls refused. Both native floor trials and the GLB remained reproducible after restart.
